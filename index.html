About these explorers
Data. Tasic 2018 (mouse VISp + ALM SMART-seq full-transcriptome). Subclass HVG selected on log-CPM via Seurat-v3 variance. Curated curated-gene-set buttons add receptor/neuropeptide overlays not always in the HVG top-N.

Recompute. Every "recompute" view refits its embedding entirely in the browser on whichever cells / genes you've selected. SVD uses power-iteration with deflation; UMAP is in-browser umap-js; diffusion map is a Laplacian eigenmap; NMF is multiplicative- update (Lee & Seung) with K=4 archetypes embedded in a tetrahedron.

Metabolism filter. Per-gene Pearson with %ribosomal is computed at build time. The slider hides every gene above a |r| threshold (default 1.0 = off). Most ribo- correlated genes are uniform-expression housekeeping (median subclass-CV 0.027 vs 0.477 for the rest of the transcriptome); excluding them removes a technical axis without losing biological structure. The "Regress out %ribo" toggle is the projection- based alternative — present in every recompute view.

Sharing. Click 🔗 Copy link top-right and the URL hash encodes every control. Sharing a URL reproduces the exact view; the page auto-fires the same Recompute you last clicked. Clicking a View: button at the top of an explorer carries the current state across to the next viz.
Source: Tasic et al. 2018 (mouse VISp + ALM SMART-seq) · Zhuang ABCA-1/3 (MERFISH spatial) · CCFv3 25 µm parcellation from Allen Brain Atlas · Yao 2023 taxonomy. Built with Plotly, umap-js, scanpy, scikit-image, alphashape, shapely. Self-contained HTML, no server.
