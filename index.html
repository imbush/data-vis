About these explorers
Datasets. Adult V1 + ALM SMART-seq (Tasic et al. 2018); developmental V1 10x atlas E11.5 → P56 (Gao et al. 2025, Allen ABC Atlas); patch-seq morpho-electric- transcriptomic cells from VISp (Gouwens et al. 2020) and M1 (Scala et al. 2021), K-NN-imputed onto the Tasic reference and overlaid on its SVD; and Zhuang et al. ABCA MERFISH for the spatial view. Per-subclass HVG selected on log-CPM via Seurat-v3 variance; curated gene-set buttons add receptor / neuropeptide overlays beyond the HVG top-N.

Four embeddings, recomputed in-browser. Each explorer refits its embedding client-side on whichever cells (subtype / lineage / region / age filters) and genes you select. SVD = power-iteration with deflation; UMAP = umap-js; diffusion map = Laplacian eigenmap; NMF = multiplicative-update (Lee & Seung), K=4 archetypes in a tetrahedron. Switch method from the Plotting Method box; the plots sit on top, controls below.

Colour. Cells colour by subtype by default (cardinal classes share a hue family across every dataset); the Color Scheme box also colours by QC counts / genes / %ribo, pseudotime, dissection layer, or any gene's expression (hover a gene). In the patch-seq views the overlaid cells recolour from their own transcriptome and re-project live when you recompute.

Metabolism filter. Per-gene Pearson with %ribosomal is computed at build time; the slider hides genes above a |r| threshold (1.0 = off) and "Regress out %ribo" projects it out — both strip a technical housekeeping axis without losing biological structure.

Sharing. 🔗 Copy link encodes every control in the URL hash, so a shared link reproduces the exact view and re-fires your last recompute.
Data: Tasic et al. 2018 (V1 + ALM SMART-seq) · Gao et al. 2025 (developmental V1, Allen ABC Atlas) · Gouwens et al. 2020 & Scala et al. 2021 (patch-seq) · Zhuang ABCA (MERFISH spatial, CCFv3 25 µm parcellation) · Yao 2023 taxonomy. Built with Plotly, umap-js, scanpy, scikit-image, alphashape, shapely. Self-contained HTML, no server.
